Resource Profile: Home Hospitalization Careset Encounter

Official URL: http://hl7belgium.org/fhir/patient-monitoring/StructureDefinition/homehosp-encounter Version: 0.1.0
Draft as of 2026-05-18 Computable Name: HomehospEncounter
Other Identifiers: OID:2.16.840.1.113883.4.642.40.54.42.13

Profile on the FHIR Encounter resource for use in OPAT and ONCO home hospitalization caresets. The Encounter records whether the home visit took place and the status of each ordered care activity. Only hospital-ordered procedures should be reported back via this Encounter. See logical model: CarestEncounter

Usages:

  • Refer to this Profile: Home Hospitalization Careset Questionnaire Response
